Your prefab brought its physics with it

Every case mod starts as a copy of a vanilla prefab, and the prefab carries GPU clearance, radiator sizes, drive bays and motherboard support. Those numbers still describe the case you copied. The shop will sell yours without complaint while its spec sheet is a lie.

It tells you which ones you missed

Every field is diffed against its donor. Content changes are split from identity changes, so what you are looking at is genuinely what you forgot — not the ID and Part Name you were always going to change.
